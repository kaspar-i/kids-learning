## 1. How to read this tree

**Tiers = eras** (R4 C1, extended by two coarse tiers):

| Tier | Era | Age band | Estonian stage (CR14) | Detail level |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | **Spark** | 5–6 | Lasteaed (middle year) | Full (21 nodes) |
| 1 | **Foundations** | 6–7 | Koolieelik — final lasteaed year | Full (20 nodes) |
| 2 | **Builder** | 7–8 | Grade 1 (school year 1) | Full (18 nodes) |
| 3 | **Explorer** | 8–9 | Grade 2 (school year 2) | Full (29 nodes, incl. 9 career quests) |
| 4 | **Voyager** | ~10–13 | Grades ~4–7 (II–III kooliaste); grade-3 checkpoint (~9–10) sits at the 3/4 boundary | Coarse (11 nodes) |
| 5 | **Horizons** | ~14–18 | III kooliaste → gymnasium / vocational | Coarse (10 nodes) |

Estonian children enter grade 1 at 7 (CR14): tier 1 is the koolieelik year, tiers 2–3 map onto school years 1–2, and the Estonian grade-3 checkpoint (~age 9–10) sits at the tier-3/4 boundary — reachable up to a year early where mastery allows, never demanded early. Bands calibrate difficulty; mastery gates progression, never age (GP1). Tier 0–3 nodes obey the granularity rule (R4 C2) — one assessable can-do statement, masterable in 1–3 weeks of 10–20-minute sessions, parent-checkable in under 10 minutes — with the two bounded exception classes ruled in framework §5.2: **cluster nodes** (`cluster: true`; 2–4 same-domain sibling can-dos sharing materials, every named sub-check must pass) and **extended-fluency training grounds** (`strand: "training-ground"`; months-long by nature, carrying sub-level ticks, with the year-scale numeracy fluencies additionally split into staged nodes). Tier 4–5 nodes are deliberately coarser milestone bundles — they exist so every career wonder has a visible, honest path, and their decomposition now runs on a **ruled trigger, not goodwill** (framework §5.2, v1.6): when the child's frontier first enters tier 4 in any branch, that branch's bundles are decomposed within one quarter — `t4-english-fluent` first wherever the trigger fires, because it is the steepest un-staged middle and carries the flagship strand — and until then every tier-4 bundle carries interim sub-level ticks so the poster keeps moving through the ages 8–10 band where motivation is most fragile (R2 §9.4).

**Domains** (branch columns on the poster, framework §6): `numeracy`, `literacy` (Estonian + English sub-tracks sharing one phonological root, GP10), `logic`, `science`, `social-emotional`, `executive-function`, `communication`, `digital`, `motor-spatial`, `creativity`. EF and SEL are trunk, not decoration (GP16) — they carry real nodes with real checks.

**Edges** are hard prerequisites only (AND; fan-in ≤ 2 everywhere except one noted tier-5 node at 3, under the R4 C3.2 cap). Every edge passed the load-bearing test: *could the child plausibly do B well without A?* **OR-groups** (R4 C3.2) are permitted by the schema but unused through v1.7: instead, the would-be funnel nodes are specified activity-agnostically — `dig-beebot-arrows` is satisfiable with a paper grid and zero hardware, `dig-scratchjr` accepts any introductory block-based environment (ScratchJr, Scratch's junior mode, a Code.org pre-reader course), `cre-make-own-puzzle` accepts any puzzle type — so no single hated app, hated activity or missing device can block a branch (R4 C3.4's redundancy intent). A **bridge edge** (defined mechanically in framework §6) is a cross-branch hard edge *excluding* (a) the EF trunk's few out-edges (`ef-plan-check → cre-iterate-v2`, `ef-multistep-projects → sel-team-roles` — domain-general corequisites of project work, GP16), (b) named developmental corequisites, listed by edge: MOT transcription (`mot-letter-formation → lit-writing-sentences`, `mot-handwriting-auto → lit-composition`, `mot-letter-formation → dig-typing`), NUM-measurement→SCI (`num-measure-data → sci-measure-record`), and the LOG↔DIG unplugged computational-thinking spine **in both directions** (`log-sorting-patterns → dig-robot-game`, `dig-beebot-arrows → log-decompose-debug`, `log-decompose-debug → dig-scratch`), and (c) fan-in to career quest nodes and tier-4/5 milestone bundles (capstones sit above the domains and deliberately draw on many branches). So counted, v1.7 carries exactly **two bridges of 137 edges ≈ 1.5%** (cap ~10–15%), listed by name so the count is reproducible: `lit-letter-knowledge → mot-letter-formation` and `mot-letter-formation → log-sudoku-symbols`; raw cross-domain edges run ~25% (34 of 137). (Loop 3's two added edges — `spa-build-diagram → spa-scale-drawings`, `dig-scratchjr → dig-block-projects` — are both intra-branch; the re-parented edges into `t4-design-build` and `dig-scratch` likewise stay inside their branches; v1.5's six added edges all land in `quest-youtuber`, `t4-media-production` and `t5-creator-business` — quest and tier-4/5 fan-in, excluded under (c); and v1.6's seven added edges are all intra-branch (`mot-gross-body → mot-gross-2`, `sci-observe-senses → sci-body-health`, `lit-reading-fluency → lit-reads-for-fun`, `num-measure-data → num-chance`, `dig-block-projects → dig-media-sense`) or quest/tier-5 fan-in under (c) (`sci-body-health → quest-doctor`, replacing `sci-measure-record → quest-doctor`; `num-chance → t5-advanced-math`); and v1.7's single added edge (`sel-selfcare → sel-contribution`) is intra-branch SEL — so the bridge list was re-derived after each revision and is unchanged.) The named bridge list is re-checked in §7 validation. Soft "helps-with" edges — including the two R3 consensus links `sorting/patterning → counting principles` and `oral language → phonological awareness`, kept deliberately soft here so counting and phonemic awareness remain roots, and the two demoted sudoku warm-up links `log-sorting-patterns ⇢ log-sudoku-4x4` and `ef-attention-persist ⇢ log-sudoku-4x4` (see the tier-0 walkthrough) — and sensitive-period glow windows (CR9) are poster-layer decoration, deferred to the poster spec.

**Two boss milestones** mark the era boundaries, per the five-system consensus (framework §3): `num-addsub-20` (fluent +/− within 20, ~7 — completable during the koolieelik year *or* grade 1, no deadline, CR14) closes Foundations; `num-times-tables` (one-digit ×/÷ retrieval, ~9) closes Explorer. **Era-gate semantics (framework §3, binding):** a boss milestone is a *ceremonial era marker* plus an *ordinary hard prerequisite inside its own numeracy chain* — never a cross-domain gate **at branch level, a scoped rule**: within tiers 0–3, no non-numeracy *branch* node ever waits on a boss. Career quest nodes and tier-4/5 milestone bundles are explicitly carved out (mirroring the bridge-cap carve-out): they sit above the domains and legitimately inherit the numeracy chain — `quest-entrepreneur` needs money sense, `t4-science-method` needs data handling — so a boss in their ancestry is correct, not a violation; tooling must scope the check accordingly. The next era's poster goes up when the child's frontier first crosses into that tier in any branch; the ceremony celebrates and withholds nothing. The Estonian grade-3 outcome list is the compact ~9–10 external checkpoint (§5.4, CR14).

**Careers are wonders, not gates** (GP12, CR12). Each career object carries `capstone_prereqs` (its tier-5 vestibule set — the career's real sink-node prerequisites) and `quest` (its tier-3 capstone quest node). **Path-highlighting is computed from the graph plus one named constant:** for every career, the highlighted set = **ancestor closure of (`quest` ∪ `capstone_prereqs`) ∪ `common_trunk`**. The age-8, child-sized answer ("what do I need to open Doctor?") uses the `quest` closure ∪ trunk (the wonder's first door); the long horizon uses the `capstone_prereqs` closure ∪ trunk. The **`common_trunk`** is a named, ancestor-closed list of 26 node ids in the JSON below — the EF chain, SEL core, COM chain, literacy roots with **both fluency legs in full** — Estonian (`lit-ee-decoding → lit-reading-fluency`) *and* English (`lit-en-phonics-1/2`, the oral leg through `lit-en-oral-3`, and `lit-en-reading`) — and the numeracy chain through both boss gates: R4 B2's seven shared foundations. The English strand is trunk *by necessity*: it is the one strand school won't supply (GP10) and one of CR14's two scheduled run-aheads, so every child walks it regardless of wonder — a trunk without it would show six of nine careers needing zero English decoding, the exact display lie the trunk was invented to prevent. (`t4-english-fluent` deliberately stays *outside* the trunk: the trunk is scoped to the fully decomposed tiers 0–3, and the tier-4/5 layer reaches career highlights through each career's own closure — software and research careers already contain it via hard edges; the question is revisited when tiers 4–5 decompose.) It exists because the nine computed closures are career-specific *by design* and their intersection is verifiably empty — closures alone would tell a child that Lawyer needs no math and Doctor needs no self-regulation, contradicting GP12/TR7; the trunk union is what makes every highlighted path tell the truth. R4 B2's ~80%-shared-foundations finding therefore lives in the **schedule** (every child walks the whole trunk regardless of which wonder pulls them) and in this display union — not in edge reachability. (One deliberate scoping, made explicit in v1.7 per the Singapore review's S-1 trunk question: the trunk's numeracy leg caps at the `num-times-tables` boss gate, so the remaining grade-3 checkpoint nodes — `num-multidigit`, `num-problems-data`, `num-fractions` — sit outside the display union; that is safe because **the schedule carries them**: CR14's numeracy run-ahead is walked by every child regardless of wonder, so the checkpoint content reaches every child by schedule, and this sentence is where the schedule says so.) The per-node `careers` arrays are advisory *heaviest-feed* authoring tags (R4 B3) — deliberately not ancestor-closed, and never used for highlighting, gating **or poster coloring**: they carry tag/ancestry mismatches by construction, so coloring from them would paint career colors onto nodes whose feeder chains carry none; poster coloring derives from the same computed closure ∪ trunk sets. A tenth blank "design your own" wonder slot (and its quest) ships on the poster but is intentionally absent from this graph. Wild nodes (CR6) likewise live on the poster, not here: this file is the engineered trunk.

**Mastery** follows the state machine of framework §5.1 (Locked → Available → Practicing → Mastered → Consolidated → Needs-polish); only Consolidated satisfies a prerequisite, with four scoped §5.1 rulings: **intra-tier-0 edges are satisfied at Mastered** (the entry band carries no hidden multi-week consolidation tax); **quests and month-scale dispositional nodes are single-shot** (Consolidated = Mastered at completion — single-shot in *graph state*: voluntary seasonal/annual quest re-runs are a poster-layer tradition since v1.7, framework §5.1's quest-revival ruling, never a state change); **Needs-polish continues to satisfy prerequisites** (decay dims the poster, never re-locks it); and a **batched placement sweep** lets a child entering with existing skills reach their real frontier within a week or two, with placement-passed nodes marked Consolidated directly. Checks use the two archetypes of framework §5.2: performance checks by default; naturalistic **observation tallies** (≥3 unprompted instances across ≥2 weeks) for dispositional SEL/EF nodes — with two v1.7 register scopings: owned-job sub-checks tally per **completed rota cycle**, not per unprompted instance, and Band-1 (tier-0) Archetype-A checks run in the **observed-or-played register** (framework §5.2, v1.7). Per-node mastery-check specs, Eureka quests and decay timers are authored per node in the booklet pipeline, not duplicated here (the two boss nodes carry theirs inline as `mastery_check` because tooling gates eras on them).

## 2. The nine career end-goals

**Doctor 🩺** — heaviest on literacy, numeracy, EF and SEL (R4 B3). A doctor reads dense technical text for decades, holds multi-step protocols in working memory under stress, executes fine-motor work with precision, measures and interprets bodily data, and — decisively — pairs calm inhibitory control ("yuck/scary" doesn't shake the hands) with genuine empathy and perspective-taking. The age-5–9 seed corpus: reading stamina, living/non-living and body vocabulary (the body-and-health rung `sci-body-health`, real since v1.6 — the claim previously had no node under it), measurement, multi-step instructions from memory, pencil-to-scalpel motor precision, the science observation trunk, and the SEL chain from naming feelings to reading them in others.

**Software engineer / IT specialist 💻** — heaviest on logic, numeracy, EF and digital. The working day is decomposition (break a problem into steps), pattern recognition, constraint reasoning, and above all debugging — the emotional skill of treating a wrong answer as information. The tree's signature on-ramp: picture sudoku (constraint satisfaction with zero literacy load) plus the unplugged→Bee-Bot→ScratchJr→Scratch chain (CR5), riding on math fluency and long-attention EF.

**Fortune-500 manager / executive 🏢** — heaviest on SEL, communication, EF and numeracy. Management is deciding with incomplete information, explaining the decision in three clear sentences, reading the room, delegating with role clarity, and running plan→do→debrief loops (OECD's Anticipation–Action–Reflection). Seeds: turn-taking and role allocation in play, planning before doing, explaining a choice, resource budgeting ("you have 10 tokens"), the full SEL conflict-and-collaboration chain, and goal-setting with honest self-review.

**Lawyer ⚖️** — heaviest on literacy, communication, logic and EF. Law is precise reading of dense text, retelling with the sequence intact, giving reasons ("I think X *because*…"), spotting rule violations, fairness reasoning, and listening-then-summarizing the other side before answering it. Seeds: the comprehension chain (who/what/why), the "because" muscle trained on sudoku explanations, rule-games, and structured argument culminating in debate and mock trial.

**Scientist / researcher 🔬** — heaviest on logic, numeracy, literacy and creativity. Science is disciplined curiosity: observe closely, sort and classify, measure, predict-test-record with one variable changed at a time, chart results, explain with evidence, and write it up. The thin observation trunk runs from age 5 (Singapore proof: habits before content, framework §6 SCI); content bursts join from 7; the fair test closes the band; the independent research project (uurimistöö) closes schooling.

**Engineer (civil/mechanical) 🏗️** — heaviest on motor-spatial, numeracy, logic and creativity. Engineering runs on spatial ability — which predicts STEM over and above math and verbal (Wai) and is highly trainable at 5–9 — plus measurement, structural intuition, reading and producing technical drawings, and iterate-after-failure ("version 2 held the coins"). Spatial is a trunk here, not a leaf (TR6): blocks, tangrams, symmetry, build-from-diagram, maps, then real design-build-test cycles on the Nordic tööõpetus asset.

**Entrepreneur 🚀** — heaviest on creativity, SEL, communication and numeracy. The profile: idea fluency (ten uses for a box), noticing problems worth solving, simple unit economics (profit = sell − cost), persuading, leading a tiny team, and — the differentiator — metabolizing a flopped idea without flinching. Seeds: the create-step of every learning loop (GP7), make-your-own puzzle culture, money sense, the v1→v2 iteration habit, and a real student company (õpilasfirma) at the top.

**Finance professional 📈** — heaviest on numeracy, EF, logic and literacy. Finance needs deep number sense and place value, ratio and percent instincts ("is the big pack actually cheaper?"), probability intuition (seeded at `num-chance` since v1.6 — the claim previously had no node before age 14), delayed gratification made visible (savings-jar charts), fluent reading of tables and statements, and spreadsheet-grade systematic checking — the same discipline picture sudoku trains as elimination and candidate marks. The spine is the whole numeracy chain, capped by ledgers, models and analysis.

**YouTuber / Content Creator 🎬** — heaviest on communication, creativity, EF and digital, with SEL's audience empathy close behind. The working craft: find a story worth telling, script it, say it to a camera clearly and warmly, shoot and edit until version 2 is genuinely better, publish on a schedule actually kept, read audience feedback without flinching, and run the channel as a small honest venture — branding, analytics, monetization. Online safety is a first-class, parent-gated strand of this wonder, not fine print: publishing stays inside the family gate throughout childhood (the DIG branch's e-safety thread, threaded from first screen contact), and the gate lifts only as the child's own platform judgment is demonstrated at the top of the tree. The viewer is trained before the creator: audience judgment as a *consumer* — what's an ad, who made this, what the app gets when you keep watching — is a real node (`dig-media-sense`, v1.6), not fine print. Seeds at 5–9: the retell-to-present storytelling chain, giving reasons and explaining decisions aloud, idea fluency and the v1→v2 iteration habit, multi-day project discipline and goal charts, and the block-coding line's storyboard-build-premiere culture.

## 3. Tier walkthroughs

### Tier 0 — Spark (5–6): seventeen roots, twenty-one nodes

Guided-play register, zero English decoding demanded (GP10, GP11), cut-out tiles and 2 cm grids (B3). Every domain plants its root: counting-with-cardinality and subitising before any arithmetic (GP18), oral phonemic awareness before any print (the "true root node of all literacy"), English listening from day one (`lit-en-oral-vocab` — the strand GP10 says "runs hard from 5"), sorting/patterning before constraint puzzles, senses-based observation, the robot game and its arrow-program continuation as unplugged computing (Bee-Bot optional, paper grid canonical — the ProgeTiiger kindergarten schedule, CR5), form drawing and blocks for the motor-spatial trunk, a gross-motor observation cluster (`mot-gross-body`: balance, hops, big-ball catch — the koolivalmidus playground half of MOT-SPA's "gross before fine", tracked as observation tallies and deliberately never a hard gate on desk work), and the EF/SEL pair (10-minute persistence; four named feelings, now with a calming-toolkit sub-check closing the framework §6 co-regulation→self-regulation rung) that everything else leans on. **The v1.6 life stratum plants its tier-0 roots here too:** a practical-life/self-care cluster (`sel-selfcare` — dressing, tidying own materials, a daily table contribution: the Montessori pillar and literal koolivalmidus criteria the graph had dropped), a safety-and-wellness cluster (`sel-safe-well` — real-walk traffic rules, the reflector habit, the stranger/lost script with own address, pool rules; health rhythms ride booklet Life pages), and a rhythm-and-song cluster (`cre-music-play` — steady beat, call-and-response, songs and 2–3 Estonian rhymes/poems by heart: the home-sized answer to *muusika* and the Estonian songs-and-poems carrier, framework CR17). All three are Archetype-B observation clusters that gate no desk work, ever. Seventeen nodes have no prerequisites (the roots) — **including `log-sudoku-4x4`, the home node of entry booklets 1a and 1b** (framework §4.4 note 4): the entry point ships as a *pair* of thin booklets so B7's one-new-mechanic-per-booklet rule holds for a day-1 five-year-old — **1a** is self-contained (its opening pages *are* the sorting-and-patterns content, and its single new mechanic is S1's no-repeats-in-a-row rule on strips), **1b** adds S2's single new mechanic (columns join rows on the 4×4) — so the line legitimately starts on day 1. Its former hard edges from sorting and attention are demoted to poster-layer helps-with links: hard-requiring a Consolidated observation tally locked the flagship booklet for ~a month after day 1 and was circular besides, since GP16 trains attention *through* sudoku-class games. The remaining intra-tier edges are few — bonds-of-5 needs counting + subitising; letter knowledge needs sound games; arrow programs need the robot game — and are satisfied at **Mastered**, not Consolidated (framework §5.1 tier-0 exception), so the entry band never hides a multi-week wait.

### Tier 1 — Foundations (6–7): first formal strands, earned

The koolieelik year. The two evidence-mandated explicit strands switch on, visually flagged as "training grounds" (CR1, TR9): English synthetic phonics on a UK-style SSP sequence — begun at 6–7 by our own ruling, a deliberately delayed start relative to UK Reception's statutory 4–5 that reaches the same endpoint by ~9 (dose adequacy argued and quarterly-monitored in framework §5.4: 1:1 dosing plus older-starter efficiency, with a third weekly phonics session as the lever if GPC coverage falls more than a phase behind), and one of CR14's two *scheduled* run-aheads of the Estonian timetable (the other is the numeracy consensus clock) — and number-fact retrieval, staged as bonds-to-10 → within-10 → within-20 so the tree moves while fluency grows (§5.2 extended-fluency ruling). Teen-numbers-as-ten-and-ones is its own small node — the genuinely load-bearing piece for crossing ten. English listening gets its tier-1 continuation (`lit-en-oral-2`) so the oral leg of the Simple View never idles. The box rule joins sudoku on 4×4 (S3); the first block-coding node marks first deliberate, co-used screen contact (CR5; any introductory block environment satisfies it). The tier-1 EF node `ef-plan-check` is trunk by schedule, session design and the `common_trunk` union (GP16) — every branch's sessions exercise plan-do-check, and every career highlight includes it — while its hard out-edges stay deliberately few and project-shaped. The gross-motor line no longer dead-ends at tier 0: `mot-gross-2` (v1.6) watches bike riding, water confidence anticipating Estonia's **compulsory I-kooliaste beginner swimming course** (school-delivered, framework CR17 — this is the home-side readiness watch), and seasonal skills — observation-only, with no consumers, so the MOT column keeps a playground half after age six without ever gating desk work. The life stratum grows its **contribution rung** here too (v1.7, Japan review J-1): `sel-contribution` — a rotating weekly family job owned end-to-end, shared-space reset beyond own materials, a simple family food element weekly (maturing toward ~8), and a regular small service *to a person* — an Archetype-B cluster on the §5.2 rota-reliability semantics (a completed rota cycle is the tally unit, not an unprompted instance), with zero consumers and no desk-work gating, its jobs assigned at the weekly family meeting (framework CR20); the family is this system's community of contribution, and this is the rung where the child becomes a serving member of it rather than only its audience. The era closes at the first consensus boss gate — **fluent +/− within 20** — which may equally close during grade 1 (CR14; koolivalmidus itself expects only ± within 5).

### Tier 2 — Builder (7–8): school register, tools and money

Grade 1 begins. Reading becomes a tool (Estonian fluency; English digraphs and decodable mini-texts), handwriting automatizes to free working memory for composing, multiplicative thinking enters as equal groups and arrays — concept before drill, the defensible order per the five-system comparison (R3 §5 multiplication-timing row); table *retrieval* waits for tier 3 — money and time become real, the 0–100 linear number line consolidates inside place-value work, sudoku crosses the picture→symbol bridge on 6×6 with candidate marks (S4; B4, B9), block coding deepens into a paper-planned multi-sprite project with own-bug fixing (`dig-block-projects` — the tier-2 digital rung that keeps the first-screen skill warm instead of letting the 8–12-week decay timer dim it for the year between ScratchJr and Scratch) while decomposition-and-debugging goes explicit, and projects stretch across days. The science trunk gains its body-and-health rung (`sci-body-health`, v1.6): body parts and the senses as organs, healthy habits in the child's own words, three-step first aid, and a weeks-long care-for-a-living-thing log — closing the five-system age-6 "body parts & senses" consensus milestone, the *loodusõpetus/inimeseõpetus* health slice, and the doctor career's previously undelivered seed corpus; the human body joins the SCI content cycles where R3's consensus always placed it.

### Tier 3 — Explorer (8–9): fluency, evidence, audience — and the first career doors

Grade 2. The second boss gate — **one-digit ×/÷ retrieval**, staged as first-tables (2, 5, 10, 3, 4) then the full korrutustabel — plus numbers to 10,000, fractions from fair shares to the number line, and two-step bar-model problems. This runs ahead of Estonian grade 2 *by design*: numeracy is the second of CR14's two scheduled run-aheads, on the international consensus clock (R3 §6), so tier 3 carries the Estonian grade-3 checkpoint list as *available* target content — banded 8–9, due never; the official checkpoint sits at ~9–10 and completing its list across the tier-3/4 boundary is normal (the four checkpoint nodes say so on their own cards). English reading goes independent on *both* legs — decoding and language comprehension (Simple View, GP10). Composition gets plan-draft-edit; the fair test completes the inquiry ladder; Scratch replaces the block-projects line; typing starts; spatial work steps up to scale drawings, nets and top/front/side views (`spa-scale-drawings` — the tier-3 rung the engineer spine needs, added in loop 3 precisely because 8–9 is the band where spatial training pays most and the branch previously skipped it); creativity continues through invent-and-improve; teams get named roles; presentations get audiences. Three v1.6 additions land here: reading *appetite* gets seen, not just decoding (`lit-reads-for-fun` — a dispositional Archetype-B node that never gates and never carries a reward, GP9); chance reasoning gets its childhood seed (`num-chance` — spinner/dice games feeding `t5-advanced-math`'s probability lab, making the finance profile's claim true); and the e-safety thread grows its viewer side (`dig-media-sense` — ad-or-content, who-made-this-and-why, the family watch-time agreement — so the youtuber wonder trains the viewer before the creator). And the wonder-pull design lands its payload: **nine career capstone quest nodes unlock here** (CR12) — teddy clinic, game night, restaurant night, family court, kitchen lab, bridge challenge, market stall, pocket-money ledger, family show — each a week-to-month-scale, child-sized first door into its wonder (the ledger and goal charts genuinely run a month — §5.1's single-shot ruling says so), with the design-your-own tenth on the poster. An 8-year-old asking "what do I need to open Doctor?" gets a path to `quest-doctor`, not to gymnasium biology.

### Tier 4 — Voyager (~10–13): coarse milestone bundles

Eleven bundles: algebra-and-data over arithmetic, research writing, English as a working language, text-based programming, controlled experiments, debate, money management, leading a team, design-build-test, media production (shoot-edit-tell — the presenting and block-projects lines continued into media craft), and first ventures (the creativity/entrepreneurship continuation). Each is the obvious continuation of a completed tier-3 chain; each will be decomposed into real nodes later. (Not one-per-strand: numeracy, literacy and digital carry two each, logic folds into programming, EF into leading-teams — the walkthrough claims only what the data shows.)

### Tier 5 — Horizons (~14–18): career vestibules

Ten vestibule nodes: advanced math, life sciences, shipping software, independent research (uurimistöö), argumentation and law, a real student venture (õpilasfirma), sustained leadership, applied engineering, financial analysis, and a creator channel run as a real venture. Careers are now real sinks: each career's `capstone_prereqs` names the vestibule set that opens it (AND semantics), and every vestibule chain bottoms out in tier-0 roots (validated, §6). Breadth per R4 C3.4 is stated honestly rather than oversold: every career's capstone ancestry contains at least two tier-5 vestibules except **lawyer** (`t5-argument-law` only) and **entrepreneur** (`t5-venture` only) — and since every edge is AND, each single vestibule's two tier-4 legs are *both mandatory*. These are **chokepoints, accepted as design** at this coarse layer: structured argument is to law what programming is to software, and no honest alternative route around the competency itself exists. The one-hated-activity protection (R4 C3.4) operates at *format* level instead — the bundles are format-agnostic (any debate club, mock-trial group or structured-argument practice satisfies `t4-debate`; any repeated make-price-sell cycle satisfies `t4-venture-lab`; any multi-source report format satisfies `t4-academic-writing`) — and when tiers 4–5 are decomposed into real nodes, genuine alternatives will get OR-groups, which the schema already permits. The coarse-layer gaps are named rather than papered over — and since v1.6 the admission is applied consistently, not only where loop 3 happened to look: `t5-financial-analysis` promises spreadsheet modeling, `t5-applied-engineering` promises CAD/robotics, and `t5-creator-business` promises analytics read honestly, while no digital-tools or data-literacy node sits in any of those hard ancestries (seven of nine careers currently reach 18 with zero DIG nodes in their closure — only the software and creator paths carry DIG); likewise `t5-life-sciences` carries medicine's people-facing SEL leg in its exercise list with no SEL ancestry, and `t5-venture` demands a pitch while `com-present` is enrichment for the entrepreneur. A spreadsheet/data-tools rung enters the money-management and design-build chains, and a service/SEL leg the doctor vestibule, when tiers 4–5 decompose on the framework §5.2 trigger — and until then **each of those five capstone cards says so itself** instead of implying its chain teaches the missing piece.

## 5. Structural invariants (enforced, not aspirational)

1. The graph is a DAG; prerequisites only point same-tier-or-earlier, and intra-tier edges never form cycles.
2. Every non-tier-0 node has ≥1 prerequisite; every zero-prerequisite node sits in tier 0 — so every career chain bottoms out in Spark.
3. Hard fan-in ≤ 2 per node in tiers 0–4; exactly one tier-5 node (`t5-research-project`) uses fan-in 3, inside R4 C3.2's cap of 3.
4. Node total: 109 (careers excluded). Tiers 0–3 hold 88 — below R4 C1's ~120–160 sketch by design (the same figure CR13 cites; v1.2's "110–150" traced to no document and is corrected, as v1.0's "50–80" was before it), via the cluster-node ruling (§5.2) that keeps sibling can-dos in one poster cell; the total is consistent with that ~120–160 full-system budget once wild nodes, poster variants and decomposed tier-4/5 nodes are added later.
5. Longest chain *within* any single tier is 3 nodes (budget ≤ 6, R4 C3.4 — a tier reads as conquerable; e.g. tier 1's bonds-10 → within-10 → within-20); the longest cross-tier spine through tiers 0–3 is 9 nodes (counting → bonds-5 → bonds-10 → +/−10 → +/−20 → groups → first-tables → tables → problems), spread over four years.
6. Node-level `careers` tags are advisory authoring metadata — never gating, never used for highlighting, never rendered as poster colors (CR12, §1); career wonders open via their own `capstone_prereqs` and tier-3 `quest` doors, and both path-highlighting and poster coloring are computed as ancestor closure ∪ `common_trunk` (§1).
7. Every career's `quest` node and every id in `capstone_prereqs` resolves, and each career's full ancestor set reaches tier-0 roots.
8. `common_trunk` ids all resolve; the set is **ancestor-closed** (every hard ancestor of a trunk node is itself in the trunk, so the union never dangles); and every career's highlighted set (closure ∪ trunk) contains at least **three tier-0 roots spanning at least three domains** — TR6's ≥3-roots-per-career rule, satisfied trivially because the trunk alone contributes roots in EF, SEL, COM, LIT and NUM.
9. Counted bridge edges (framework §6 definition, with its named exclusion lists) are exactly the two listed in §1, within the ~10–15% cap; any edit that adds a cross-branch hard edge must re-derive this list, not just the count.
