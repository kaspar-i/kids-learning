How to play

  1. Look at one row (→). Every picture must appear exactly once.
  2. Now check the column (↓) — exactly once there too.
  3. Find a dashed box and ask: which picture is missing here?
  4. Draw the missing picture (or write its number). Do this for every empty box!
  5. In the 4×4 puzzles there is one more rule: each 2×2 box with thick lines also gets every picture once.

In this row the 🐶 and the 🐱 are already there…
so the empty box must be the 🐰!

For grown-ups: one or two puzzles per sitting is plenty. If an answer is wrong, don't correct it — ask "can you check this row together with me?" and let your child find it. The booklet gets harder section by section (look at the stars); it's fine to stay in a section until it feels easy.
